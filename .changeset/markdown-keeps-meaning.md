Keep what Markdown says when it becomes LINE text. A link inside a table keeps its URL as `text (url)`, a task list keeps its `[x]` and `[ ]` checkboxes, a footnote keeps its `[1]` marker, and a reference-style image keeps its alt text. Before, `- [x] done` and `- [ ] todo` looked the same, and a table link could not be opened.
